Bio

Currently a Research Scientist @ Intel, part of the GPU ART group. I graduated with a Ph.D. from INRIA Sophia Antipolis, part of the GraphDeco team, and during my studies I interned at Nvidia. I was supervised by George Drettakis and my interests include Global Illumination, Path Tracing, Path Guiding, Neural Rendering, Gaussian Splatting and Generative Models.
